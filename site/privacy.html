Privacy

What this site collects

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking scripts. We do not ask you to create an account.

The prototype demo

When you use “Try the prototype”, the text you type is sent to Anthropic's Claude API so it can be sorted and a reply can be drafted. The result is shown to you in the page and is not saved by Hermes Desk. Anthropic processes the text under its own commercial terms and privacy policy.

Please do not type real names, phone numbers, addresses or other personal details into the demo. Use example messages instead.

When Hermes Desk is used with real customers

Hermes Desk is in development. Before it handles real customer conversations for anyone other than our founder, we will publish a fuller policy covering what is stored, for how long, and how customers can ask for their data to be deleted.
