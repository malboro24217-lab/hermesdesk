In Vietnamese real estate, buyers, sales agents and billing all run through chat. One coordinator sits in the middle: answering new buyers, chasing agents, sending deposit bills and calming complaints, all in the same thread of messages.

When that inbox gets busy, a hot lead waits an hour, an angry buyer waits a day, and a bill gets forgotten. These numbers come from our founder's own working day.

How it works

  1. Read

    Hermes Desk reads each incoming message in Vietnamese, along with the conversation history for that customer.

  2. Route

    It sorts the message as a new lead, a complaint or a billing request, marks how urgent it is, and assigns it to the right person.

  3. Draft for approval

    It writes a reply in the coordinator's tone. Nothing is sent until a person approves it.

Who is building it

Hermes Desk is built by Việt Tùng, a coordinator who works every day between buyers, sales agents and billing on real estate projects in Vietnam. He handles leads, sends bills and resolves complaints across a chat inbox that runs from 8 am to 9 pm.

Hermes Desk is the tool he is building for his own desk first. Once it holds up in his daily work, it will open to other coordinators and sales teams.
